Delete your LooBook account

Fastest: in the app

  1. Open LooBook and tap your initials in the search bar.
  2. Tap Delete account, tick the box, and tap Delete.

Without the app

Email [SUPPORT EMAIL] from the address you signed up with, with the subject Delete my account. If you signed in with Apple and hid your email, include your LooBook display name. We'll confirm and delete it within 30 days.

What gets deleted

  • Your email address, sign-in details and display name
  • Your "worked / didn't work" votes, reports and blocks

What stays

  • Codes you shared stay on the map with no name attached, so others can still use them.
  • Records of tips are kept by Apple or Google as their rules require.

Backups are cleared within 90 days.
