# Privacy Policy

_Draft for review by a lawyer before launch. Last updated: [DATE]._

This policy explains what information LooBook collects, why, and the choices you have. "We" means [COMPANY NAME].

## What we collect

- **Account details.** Your email address, display name, and a sign-in ID from Apple or Google if you use those. If you use Sign in with Apple and choose "Hide my email", we only receive a private relay address.
- **Content you post.** Codes, notes, confirmations ("worked" or "didn't work") and reports, with the time you posted them.
- **Location.** Only if you allow it, and only while the app is open. We use it to centre the map and show nearby places. We do not track you in the background, and we do not store a history of where you have been.
- **Device and usage data.** App version, device type, crash reports, and basic usage events (for example, "opened a place"), so we can fix bugs and improve the app.

## How we use it

- To run the map and show codes near you.
- To show who added a code and when (by display name only).
- To prevent spam and abuse, and to review reports.
- To contact you about your account or changes to our policies.

We do **not** sell your personal information, and we do not use it for third-party advertising.

## Who we share it with

- **Service providers** that host our data and send email (for example, our database and hosting provider), under contracts that limit how they use it.
- **Other users** see your display name next to codes you add. They never see your email or location.
- **The law.** We may share information when required by law or to protect people's safety.

## How long we keep it

We keep your account details until you delete your account. When you delete it, we erase your personal information within 30 days. Codes you posted may stay on the map without your name. Backups are cleared within 90 days.

## Your choices and rights

- Turn location off at any time in your phone's settings.
- Edit your display name in Profile.
- Delete your account in Profile, then Delete account. You do not need to email us to do it.
- Depending on where you live (for example the EU, UK or California), you may have the right to access, correct, download or delete your data, or to object to certain uses. Email [PRIVACY EMAIL] and we will respond within 30 days.

## Children

LooBook is not meant for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information. If you believe a child has signed up, contact us and we will delete the account.

## Security

We use encryption in transit, access controls, and row-level security on our database. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a strong password.

## International users

We may store and process information in the United States and other countries where our providers operate.

## Changes

If we change this policy in a significant way, we will tell you in the app first.
