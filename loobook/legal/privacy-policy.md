# Privacy Policy

_Draft for review by a lawyer before launch. Last updated: [DATE]._

This policy explains what information LooBook collects, why, and the choices you have. "We" means [COMPANY NAME].

## What we collect

- **Account details.** Your email address, display name, and a sign-in ID from Apple or Google if you use those. If you use Sign in with Apple and choose "Hide my email", we only receive a private relay address.
- **Content you post.** Codes, notes, confirmations ("worked" or "didn't work") and reports, with the time you posted them. If you post a code anonymously, we still store which account posted it, but we never show it to other users.
- **Location.** Only if you allow it, and only while the app is open. We use it to centre the map and show nearby places. We do not track you in the background, and we do not store a history of where you have been.
- **Device and usage data.** App version, device type, crash reports, and basic usage events (for example, "opened a place"), so we can fix bugs and improve the app.
- **Advertising data.** Our ad partner, Google AdMob, collects your IP address and device information, and, only if you allow it, your device's advertising ID. See "Ads" below.
- **Tips.** If you leave a tip, Apple or Google processes the payment. We (through our payments provider, RevenueCat) receive a record that a purchase happened and its amount. We never see your card details.

## How we use it

- To run the map and show codes near you.
- To show who added a code and when (by display name only, and not at all for anonymous posts).
- To confirm your email address with a one-time code before your account can be used.
- To prevent spam and abuse, and to review reports.
- To contact you about your account or changes to our policies.

We do **not** sell your personal information, and we never share your precise location with advertisers.

## Ads

LooBook is free because it shows a small number of ads, supplied by Google AdMob. Ads are never placed on top of the map.

- **iPhone:** we ask for permission before your advertising ID is used ("Allow LooBook to track your activity…"). If you say no, you get ads that are not personalised.
- **Europe and the UK:** we ask for your consent with Google's consent form before any personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time in Profile, then Ad privacy choices.
- **Everyone:** you can reset or limit your advertising ID in your phone's settings.

Google's use of data is described at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

## Who we share it with

- **Service providers** that host our data and send email (Supabase), process tips (Apple, Google, RevenueCat) and serve ads (Google AdMob), under contracts that limit how they use it.
- **Other users** see your display name next to codes you add, unless you post anonymously. They never see your email, your location or how you voted.
- **The law.** We may share information when required by law or to protect people's safety.

## How long we keep it

We keep your account details until you delete your account. When you delete it, we erase your personal information within 30 days. Codes you posted may stay on the map without your name. Backups are cleared within 90 days.

## Your choices and rights

- Turn location off at any time in your phone's settings.
- Change your display name by emailing us.
- Delete your account in Profile, then Delete account. You do not need to email us to do it.
- Depending on where you live (for example the EU, UK, Brazil, Canada, California and other US states), you may have the right to access, correct, download or delete your data, to object to or restrict certain uses, and to withdraw consent. Email [PRIVACY EMAIL] and we will respond within 30 days. You can also complain to your local data protection authority.

## Legal bases (EU and UK)

We use your account details and content to provide the Service (contract). We use location and personalised ads only with your consent. We use usage data and reports to keep the Service safe and working (legitimate interests).

## Children

LooBook is not meant for children under 13 (or under the minimum age for online services where they live), and we do not knowingly collect their information. If you believe a child has signed up, contact us and we will delete the account.

## Security

We use encryption in transit, access controls, and row-level security on our database. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a strong password.

## International users

LooBook is available worldwide. We may store and process information in the United States and other countries where our providers operate. Where the law requires it, we use safeguards such as the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses for these transfers.

## Changes

If we change this policy in a significant way, we will tell you in the app first.
