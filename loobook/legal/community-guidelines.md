# Community Guidelines

LooBook only works if the codes are right and the businesses on the map are treated well. Please follow these rules.

## Share

- Codes printed on a receipt or given to customers by staff.
- Codes posted on a sign or door where anyone can see them.
- Restrooms that don't need a code. Log them as **No code needed** so they show green on the map.
- Helpful notes, such as "door is past the kitchen" or "ask for the key at the counter".

## Do not share

- Employee-only, staff room, office, alarm, safe or security codes.
- Codes from homes, apartment buildings, gyms or other private or members-only places.
- Codes you got by looking over someone's shoulder or by guessing.
- Anything rude, hateful, sexual, threatening, or advertising.
- Anyone's personal information, including names of staff.

## Keep it accurate

- Posting anonymously hides your name from other people, not from our moderators. The same rules apply.
- Tap **Worked** or **Didn't work** after you try a code. This is how everyone knows a code is still good.
- If a code has changed, use **Update code** rather than adding a second place.

## Be a good guest

Buy something when a place asks you to. Leave the restroom the way you would want to find it. If staff ask you to leave, leave.

## Reports and enforcement

Tap **Report** on any place to flag a problem. We review reports within 24 hours. Posting private codes or abusive content can get your account suspended or removed.
