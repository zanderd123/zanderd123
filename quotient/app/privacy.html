Privacy Policy

Version 2026-11-16 · Last updated 16 November 2026

Quotient is made by [DEVELOPER NAME] ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information Quotient handles, why, and the choices you have. The short version: no ads, no tracking, no selling of data. We keep only what the app needs to work.

1. Without an account

If you never create an account, your progress (answers, scores, streaks, badges, settings, puzzle notes) is stored only on your device. We don't receive it. The app's fonts and pictures are built in, so using it without an account sends nothing to us or anyone else.

If you turn on the daily reminder, it is scheduled by your phone itself; no reminder data leaves your device.

2. With an account

When you create an account we store:

  • Email address and password, to sign you in and send verification codes. Passwords are stored hashed by our sign-in provider; we never see them.
  • Proof you accepted the terms: the terms version, the time you accepted, and that you confirmed you are 13 or older.
  • A backup of your progress, so it follows you to other devices.
  • If you use Friends: your display name, a friend code, your friend list, requests, blocks, the stats you share (streak, level, Brain Score, skill ratings, daily results, puzzles solved), recent activity, cheers, and any reports you make or that are made about you.

Friends you accept can see your display name, shared stats and activity. People who aren't your friends see nothing except your display name when you send them a request. You can switch activity sharing off.

3. How we use it

Only to run Quotient: signing in, syncing progress, showing friends your results, keeping the community safe (handling reports), and replying if you contact us. We do not use your information for advertising, we do not track you across other apps or websites, and we do not sell or share it for those purposes.

4. Who processes it

  • Supabase hosts our database and sign-in, under a data processing agreement. Data is stored in [SUPABASE REGION, e.g. United States].
  • Our email provider [e.g. Resend] delivers verification codes to your email address.
  • Apple or Google deliver the app if you install it from their store, under their own privacy policies.

We may disclose information if the law requires it, or to protect someone's safety.

5. How long we keep it

  • Account, backup and friends data: until you delete your account.
  • Activity feed items: 60 days, then deleted automatically.
  • Reports: as long as needed to deal with them, at most 1 year.

6. Your choices and rights

  • Delete everything: in the app, Account → Delete my account. Your login, backup, profile, friends, stats, activity and cheers are deleted straight away. Progress stored on your own device stays until you remove the app.
  • Change or correct: change your display name and password in the app, or email us.
  • Access or a copy: email us and we'll send you your data.

Depending on where you live (for example the EU, UK or California) you may have further rights, such as to object to processing or to complain to a data protection authority. We rely on performing our agreement with you (the Terms of Use) to process account data. Contact us to use any of these rights; we'll respond within 30 days.

7. Security

Connections are encrypted (HTTPS). The database only lets each person read their own data and what their friends chose to share. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a password you don't use elsewhere.

8. Children

Quotient accounts are for people aged 13 and over. If you believe a child under 13 has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

9. Changes

If we change this policy in a significant way we'll tell you in the app. The version date at the top shows when it last changed.
