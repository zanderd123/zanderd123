CONTROLS

MOVE or ATTACK? Both shoot. The difference is whether a squadron will leave the station you gave it. MOVE flies to the spot you picked and holds there, firing at whatever comes to it. ATTACK hunts — it picks the closest enemy and flies to it, wherever that leads.

Preparation
Every battle opens paused and untimed. Position your whole fleet, set stances, then BEGIN BATTLE (↵). Orders you give now are carried out the instant it starts — and you can still change them mid-fight

Bombarding
Any ship can, but damage scales with the gun: per 100 points a Bastion does 45.7/s to the crust, a Falcon 27.6, a Wasp 15.0, an Aegis 2.3. Only while no enemy is inside range — and once locked they hold station and will not shoot back until the planet falls, so light hulls usually die before they finish
